Noorani Qaida Online

Noorani Qaida is a useful starting point for students learning to read the Quran. Our online lessons help students build Arabic letter recognition, sounds, joining letters and basic Quran reading skills.

What Students Learn

  • Arabic letters and correct sounds
  • Joining letters and reading practice
  • Basic pronunciation foundations
  • Preparation for Quran reading and Tajweed
  • Individual correction from a teacher

Kids and Adults

The course can be adapted for children, beginners and adults according to their current level and learning pace.
