How to Choose the Best Online Quran Teacher

Choosing an online Quran teacher is an important decision for both children and adults. A suitable teacher should understand the student's current reading level, explain lessons patiently and help the student improve step by step.

1. Check Quran Reading and Tajweed Knowledge

Ask how the teacher helps students pronounce Arabic letters correctly, learn the rules of Tajweed and correct recitation mistakes. Lessons should match the student's level rather than moving too quickly.

2. Choose a Lesson Format That Fits the Student

One-to-one online classes can give the teacher more time to listen to each student and provide individual feedback. For children, ask how lessons are kept focused and age-appropriate.

3. Discuss Schedule and Learning Goals

Before starting, explain whether the goal is Noorani Qaida, Quran reading, Tajweed or Hifz. Agree on a realistic schedule and ask how progress and revision will be reviewed.

4. Ask About Communication and Trial Lessons

Check how parents or adult students can communicate with the academy, ask questions and reschedule lessons. If a trial lesson is offered, use it to assess teaching style and whether the student feels comfortable.

5. Review Progress Regularly

Good learning requires consistent practice. Ask for clear feedback on strengths, areas to improve and the next learning goals. Avoid promises of instant results; progress depends on the student's starting level, attendance and practice.

Questions to Ask Before Enrolling

  • What is the teacher's experience with my student's level?
  • How are pronunciation and Tajweed mistakes corrected?
  • How long is each lesson, and how often are classes held?
  • How will progress be communicated?
  • What are the fees, cancellation rules and trial options?
