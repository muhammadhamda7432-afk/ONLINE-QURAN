Online Quran Classes for Kids

MH Online Quran Academy provides live one-to-one online Quran classes for children. Lessons are designed to be clear, patient and suitable for beginners or children who already read Quran.

What Kids Can Learn

  • Noorani Qaida and Arabic reading foundations
  • Nazrah Quran and correct pronunciation
  • Tajweed rules and recitation practice
  • Hifz Quran memorization
  • Basic Quran translation and Islamic learning

Why One-to-One Classes?

The teacher can focus on the child's reading, correct mistakes immediately and adjust the pace to the student's level.
